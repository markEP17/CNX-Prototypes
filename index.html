Cash App self-service

Account Appeal Self-Service

Customer self-service journey for filing and tracking an appeal related to an account restriction or freeze.

  • Dedicated appeal entry point in Account Settings
  • Appeal reason and customer narrative capture
  • Identity/account information confirmation
  • Supporting document upload
  • Immediate appeal number and 5–10 working day review estimate
  • Appeal status timeline from submission through decision

02
Cash App self-service

Close Account Eligibility

Guided self-service eligibility check that identifies blockers preventing account closure and directs customers to resolve them first.

  • Starts from Account & Settings
  • Checks cash, credit, investment, and gift card balances
  • Shows account-level outstanding amounts
  • Provides clear remediation actions for each blocker
  • Rechecks eligibility after balances are resolved
  • Guides customer to final account closure confirmation

03
Cash App self-service

Account Linking

Self-service flow for requesting that a current Cash App account be linked to a previous account.

  • Accessible from Account Settings
  • Checks whether the current account is fully verified
  • Collects Full Name, SSN, Address, and Cash Tag
  • Requires a valid ID upload
  • Confirms request submission and expected review timing
  • Shows a single active request with a progress tracker
